# Recipe & Menu Planner AI Specialist 🍳

Personal recipe curator and meal planning specialist that learns your unique tastes and cooking patterns over time.

## Features

- **Personal Recipe Collection** - Save and organize recipes with your notes and ratings
- **Smart Meal Planning** - Create weekly plans based on your taste patterns and schedule
- **Intelligent Shopping Lists** - Generate lists that know your pantry staples and shopping habits
- **Dietary Preference Learning** - Remember restrictions, preferences, and cooking constraints
- **Cooking Pattern Recognition** - Learn your weeknight vs. weekend cooking styles

## What Makes This Special

Unlike generic recipe apps, this specialist builds up knowledge about YOU specifically:
- Remembers which recipes you've tried and loved
- Learns your go-to weeknight meals vs. weekend cooking projects
- Tracks your dietary restrictions and family preferences
- Knows what ingredients you always keep stocked
- Understands your cooking schedule and time constraints

## Structure

- `/configuration/` - Specialist metadata and settings
- `/content/` - All specialist content and AI instructions
  - `getting-started/` - User introduction (auto-deleted after onboarding)
  - `recipes/` - Personal recipe collection (favorites + cuisine folders)
  - `meal-planner/` - Meal history and planning with templates
  - `preferences/` - Dietary restrictions, cooking style, household preferences
  - `shopping/` - Shopping lists, pantry staples, store preferences
  - `cooking-notes/` - Personal cooking discoveries and kitchen wisdom

## Getting Started

1. Import this repository into your Magic Context or AI Specialists Hub platform
2. Begin with: "Help me set up my cooking preferences"
3. Start building your personalized recipe collection and meal planning system

## Memory-Powered Cooking

This specialist becomes uniquely yours by:
- Learning your specific taste preferences and cooking patterns
- Remembering your recipe modifications and ratings
- Tracking your meal history to suggest what to cook next
- Building up knowledge of your kitchen capabilities and constraints

Perfect for home cooks who want a truly personalized cooking assistant that gets smarter over time!
