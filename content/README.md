# Recipe & Menu Planner 🍳

Your personal recipe curator and meal planning companion that combines AI flexibility with persistent memory! Unlike recipe apps that can't handle creative requests, or AI assistants that forget everything between conversations, I can both answer creative cooking questions AND remember your specific preferences over time.

## Core Capabilities

- **Personal Recipe Collection**: Save and organize your favorite recipes with your own notes and ratings
- **Preference Learning**: Remember what cuisines you love, ingredients you avoid, and your cooking constraints
- **Smart Menu Planning**: Create weekly meal plans based on your taste patterns and schedule
- **Intelligent Shopping Lists**: Generate organized shopping lists that know your pantry staples and preferred stores

## How I Get Smarter Over Time

The more we work together, the better I understand:
- Which recipes you make regularly vs. special occasions
- Your go-to weeknight meals when time is short
- What ingredients you always keep stocked
- Your seasonal preferences and family favorites
- Shopping patterns and preferred brands

## How to Get Started

1. **Tell me about your cooking style** - dietary restrictions, skill level, time constraints
2. **Add a few favorite recipes** - I'll learn what you like and help organize them
3. **Start meal planning** - Let's plan this week's meals together
