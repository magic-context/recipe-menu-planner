# Your Favorite Recipes

This folder contains your most-loved recipes - the ones you make regularly and always turn out great.

## What Goes Here

- Recipes you've tried and rated 4-5 stars
- Go-to meals for busy weeknights
- Family favorites that everyone loves
- Reliable recipes you can make without looking

## Example

When you have a recipe you love, move it here and Ruby will prioritize suggesting it for meal planning.

## Tips

- Include your personal notes about timing, modifications, or serving suggestions
- Note which occasions work best for each recipe
- Track how often you make each one
