# Simple Pasta Marinara

**Source**: Family recipe
**Prep Time**: 10 minutes
**Cook Time**: 20 minutes
**Serves**: 4
**Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Ingredients
- 1 lb pasta (spaghetti or penne)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried basil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parmesan cheese

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta according to package directions
2. Heat olive oil in large pan, add garlic
3. Add tomatoes and basil, simmer 15 minutes
4. Season with salt and pepper
5. Toss with pasta and serve with parmesan

## My Notes
- I always add a pinch of sugar to balance the acidity
- Kids love this - super reliable weeknight meal
- Makes great leftovers for lunch
- Sometimes I add ground turkey for protein

## When I Make This
- Busy weeknights when I need something quick
- When the kids request "just pasta"
- Good base for adding vegetables or meat
