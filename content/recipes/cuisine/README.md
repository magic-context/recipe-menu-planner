# Recipes by Cuisine

Organize your recipes by cuisine type to make them easy to find when you're craving a specific style of food.

## Current Cuisines

- **Italian** - Pasta, pizza, risotto, and more
- **Mexican** - Tacos, enchiladas, salsas, and Mexican favorites
- **Asian** - Stir-fries, curries, noodle dishes from across Asia
- **American** - Classic American comfort foods and regional dishes
- **Mediterranean** - Greek, Middle Eastern, and Mediterranean flavors

## Adding New Cuisines

When you start collecting recipes from other cuisines, Ruby can help you create new folders like:
- French
- Indian
- Thai
- BBQ/Grilling
- Whatever cuisines you enjoy!

## Organization Tips

- Don't worry about being too specific - "Asian" can include Chinese, Thai, Japanese, etc.
- Focus on groupings that make sense for YOUR cooking style
- Move recipes between cuisines if needed
