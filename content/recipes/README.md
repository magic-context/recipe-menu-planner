# Your Recipe Collection

This is where Ruby organizes your personal recipe collection. Unlike generic recipe apps, these are YOUR curated recipes with your personal notes, ratings, and modifications.

## Structure

- **`favorites/`** - Your top-rated recipes that you make regularly
- **`cuisine/`** - Recipes organized by cuisine type (Italian, Mexican, Asian, etc.)

## How to Use

- Add recipes you want to save and try
- Include your personal notes and modifications
- Rate recipes after you try them
- Ruby will learn your preferences and suggest recipes from YOUR collection

## Personal Touch

Every recipe here should have context:
- Why you saved it
- Any modifications you made
- How it turned out when you tried it
- When you like to make it (weeknight vs. weekend)

This becomes your personalized recipe database that grows over time!
