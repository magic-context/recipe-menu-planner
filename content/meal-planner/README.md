# Your Meal Planning Timeline

This is where Ruby tracks your meals across time - both what you've cooked (history) and what you plan to cook (future).

## Structure

- **Year folders** (2025, 2026, etc.)
  - **Month folders** (01-january, 02-february, etc.)
    - `week_overview_plans.md` - Weekly meal planning overview
    - Daily files: `YYYY-MM-DD-meals.md` - Individual day meal tracking
- **`templates/`** - Templates for consistent meal planning

## How It Works

### Planning Mode
- Use weekly overview files to plan the week ahead
- Create daily meal files for specific days
- Ruby suggests meals based on your recipe collection and preferences

### History Mode
- Look back at what you cooked and how it went
- See patterns in your cooking (what you make when)
- Learn from past meal planning successes and challenges

## Benefits

- **Pattern Recognition**: Ruby learns when you cook what types of meals
- **Seasonal Tracking**: See how your cooking changes throughout the year
- **Success Tracking**: Remember which meal plans worked well
- **Inspiration**: Look back at successful weeks for planning ideas

This timeline becomes your personal cooking history and planning tool!
