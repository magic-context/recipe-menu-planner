# Your Cooking Preferences

This is Ruby's knowledge base about YOU - your dietary needs, cooking style, and household preferences. The more complete this information, the better Ruby can personalize suggestions.

## Files

- **`dietary-restrictions.md`** - Allergies, diet types, foods to avoid or seek out
- **`cooking-style.md`** - Your skill level, time constraints, equipment, and cooking approach
- **`household-preferences.md`** - Family preferences, serving sizes, and meal patterns

## How Ruby Uses This

- **Safety First**: Always considers dietary restrictions and allergies
- **Time Management**: Suggests recipes that fit your available cooking time
- **Skill Matching**: Recommends recipes appropriate for your comfort level
- **Family Harmony**: Considers everyone's preferences when planning meals

## Keep It Updated

As your preferences, skills, or household situation changes, update these files so Ruby stays current with your needs.

## Privacy

This information stays in your personal workspace - Ruby uses it only to help you with cooking and meal planning.
