# Getting to Know Your Recipe & Menu Planner 👨‍🍳

Welcome to your personal recipe curator and meal planning companion! This isn't just another recipe app - it's an AI specialist that learns YOUR specific tastes, cooking patterns, and preferences to become uniquely yours over time.

## What Makes This Different?

**Regular Recipe Apps**: Start fresh every time. You have to search, filter, and re-explain your preferences in every session.

**Your Recipe & Menu Planner**: Builds up knowledge about YOU specifically:
- Remembers which recipes you've tried and loved
- Learns your go-to weeknight meals vs. weekend cooking projects
- Tracks your dietary restrictions and family preferences
- Knows what ingredients you always keep stocked
- Understands your cooking schedule and time constraints

## How Memory Makes This Powerful

### Your Personal Recipe Collection
Instead of endless searching, I maintain YOUR curated collection with your personal notes, ratings, and modifications. I know which pasta recipe is your family's favorite and which curry you tried once and didn't love.

### Smart Meal Planning
I don't just suggest random meals - I plan based on YOUR patterns:
- "You usually cook something quick on Wednesdays"
- "Your family loves Italian on weekends"
- "You prefer prep-ahead meals when work is busy"

### Intelligent Shopping
I generate shopping lists that know:
- Your pantry staples (you always have olive oil and garlic)
- Your preferred brands and stores
- What you typically buy together

### Learning Your Style
Over time, I understand:
- Your skill level and comfort with new techniques
- How much time you realistically have for cooking
- What flavors and cuisines you gravitate toward
- Which shortcuts work for your lifestyle

## Getting Started

Ready to make me uniquely yours? Let's begin by:

1. **Setting up your preferences** - Tell me about your cooking style, dietary needs, and household preferences
2. **Adding a few favorite recipes** - Start building your personal collection
3. **Planning this week's meals** - Let's see how memory-based planning works

The more we work together, the better I become at understanding exactly what you need for successful, enjoyable cooking!

## Ready to Begin?

Just say: **"Help me set up my cooking preferences"** or **"Let's plan this week's meals"** and we'll start building your personalized cooking companion.
