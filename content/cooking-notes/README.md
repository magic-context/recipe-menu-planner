# Your Personal Cooking Wisdom

This is where you capture your cooking discoveries, lessons learned, and kitchen insights that make you a better cook over time.

## Files

- **`tips-and-tricks.md`** - Your personal cooking discoveries and time-saving tips
- **`substitutions.md`** - Ingredient swaps that work for your taste and needs
- **`lessons-learned.md`** - What you learned from both cooking successes and failures

## Why This Matters

Cooking wisdom is personal! What works for one person might not work for another. Your notes capture:

- **Your Kitchen**: Equipment shortcuts specific to your setup
- **Your Taste**: Modifications that work for your preferences
- **Your Life**: Time-saving strategies that fit your schedule
- **Your Failures**: Valuable lessons from things that didn't work

## Growing Your Expertise

As you cook more, add discoveries like:
- Timing tricks that save you stress
- Ingredient substitutions that actually taste good
- Equipment uses you figured out
- Family-specific adaptations

Ruby can reference these notes to give you increasingly personalized cooking advice based on YOUR experience, not generic tips.
