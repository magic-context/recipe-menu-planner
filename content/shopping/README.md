# Your Shopping Intelligence

Ruby helps you shop smarter by learning your shopping patterns, preferred stores, and pantry staples.

## Files

- **`current-list.md`** - Your active shopping list (regenerated from meal plans)
- **`pantry-staples.md`** - Items you always keep stocked
- **`store-preferences.md`** - Your preferred stores, brands, and shopping patterns

## How This Helps

- **Smart Lists**: Ruby generates shopping lists that account for what you already have
- **Budget Friendly**: Avoid buying items you already stock
- **Organized Shopping**: Lists organized by your preferred store layout
- **Brand Preferences**: Includes your preferred brands and store-specific items

## Pantry Management

By tracking what you typically keep stocked, Ruby can:
- Suggest recipes using ingredients you have on hand
- Remind you when pantry staples need restocking
- Generate more accurate shopping lists

## Shopping Patterns

Ruby learns your shopping habits to:
- Suggest the best times and stores for your needs
- Group items by store sections
- Include household items you typically buy together
